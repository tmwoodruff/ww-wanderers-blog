We went to the MDR for breakfast and calm this morning. We were up and ready in time. The hours were normally 8-9:30 am. The ship was to be in port day at 12:30 pm. There was a line, and they ask for anyone that wanted to share. We were seated for a family with a sweet 10 year old girl and two older ladies. I'm not trying to sell them short. It sounded like my husband was having some interesting conversion with them, but I was on the other side and could not here well. I learned about Disney cruises, the little girl asked if we had been to Alaska, and then we talked about the parent's Germany trip since we had gone around Germany too. It was a fun breakfast. Since I was next to a child, I figured out that I could ask for a chocolate donut too. The chocolate donuts were good. We each also got a hot chocolate with a heart on top. Later in the cruise when I got another hot chocolate, I did not get a heart.
