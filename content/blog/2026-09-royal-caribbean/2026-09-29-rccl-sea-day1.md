Our first full day was a sea day. We first had breakfast at Cafe Promenade (CP). It was nice, calm, and not crowded. Though there were not many food options. We each had a cinnamon -- Travis liked it; I did not. Travis also got another pastry that turned out not so good, so we cannot remember what it was. They had uncrowded easy access to ice water, hot water for tea, and coffee. The hot water for tea was hard to get at the buffet and getting water in general was harder than I would expected on this ship.   

Our second breakfast was in the buffet. We found a table in the front by the entrance and bar. I got too many sweet rolls then saw that the guy at the next table had salmon on a bagel with cream cheese. I was not going to get that too but then decided to go back for one too. I got some bonus cream cheese; they had a sundried tomato cream cheese that I wanted to try. I was not sure it would go, but it was was good. Cinnamon and sugar donut was okay, and they had a nicer cinnamon roll than CP.

Then Travis went to the gym, and I walked the promenade deck. It was pretty, but windy. The wake and beautiful ocean.
