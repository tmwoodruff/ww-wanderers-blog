We were Punta Langosta Cruise Terminal, which is close to downtown. We have been to Cozumel port before; it is where you have to enter the shopping area on the second floor. This time they had the escalator at the beginning of the shopping area blocked off, so that you could not skip the shopping and had to walk all the way around to get to street level. This became even more important to me later when my feet were killing me when coming back due to a poor shoe choice. I brought croc-like shoes for before and after snorkeling, but they were killing my feet. After getting to street level, there were many taxis available for hire. 

After getting out the port we walked left to the Coral Statue. I loved this painted, capsized boat.
