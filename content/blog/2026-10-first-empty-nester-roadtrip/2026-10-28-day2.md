We headed to our campsite at Bayou Segnette State Park at Site 4, near New Orleans. This campsite was too expensive (we feel like camping used to be a lot cheaper). Though all of our sites had water and electricity at the site, except one that did not have water and had showers. National Parks normally do not have any of those three. This site had the WORST mosquitos. It was insane. Our van was full of mosquitos! We were killing them on the ceiling, near the light. We still have gray, dead mosquito spots on the ceiling that we have not been able to get off. Travis insisted that we clean the blood off immediately to avoid stains. We used water when it was fresh; it worked. I saw one on my arm and then killed one on the ceiling. I never wanted the mosquitos to transfer my blood to the van ceiling :( .
