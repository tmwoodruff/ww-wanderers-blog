We headed to our campsite at Bayou Segnette State Park at Site 4, near New Orleans. This campsite was too expensive (we feel like camping used to be a lot cheaper; this was more than others). Though all of our sites had water and electricity at the site, except one that did not have water, and had showers. National Parks normally do not have any of those three. This site had the WORST mosquitos. It was insane. Our van was full of mosquitos! We were killing them on the ceiling of the van, near the light. We still have gray, dead mosquito spots on the ceiling that we have not been able to get off. Travis insisted that we clean the blood off immediately to avoid stains. We used water when it was fresh; it worked. I saw one on my arm and then killed one on the ceiling. I never wanted the mosquitos to transfer my blood to the van ceiling for us to have to clean up :( . Our site seemed to be the only to have all the mosquitos. We went for a hike around the campground and did not see near as many other places. There was also a cat that adopted our campsite and was begging for food. It was a pretty cat but did get annoying. It came back in the morning, and in the morning, it followed me to the bathroom, then went off in another direction, but was sitting, waiting for me outside the bathroom when I came out. 

Travis cooked paninis tonight that I ate as much of as I could before I could not take the mosquitos anymore. They also loved the George Foreman's heat -- that he cooked them on; it was covered with them too. 

Restroom review: It was a nice bathroom. Had paper towels, vanity to put stuff on, not many bugs in bathroom, but a frog on the toilet seat once. I always had the bathroom to myself until my last trip right before we left. I did not try out the shower.

Campground review: We were close to the bathroom. Limited hiking; I think it was really to get people to the restrooms but did go over a cool marsh area where we saw an armadillo digging in the dark. Though the full experience was ruined by the mosquitos. We did not have kids with us, but the playground on our side was closed, which could be a disappointment if someone picked a site nearby where their kids could play and burn off energy close by. The playground on the other side was open.
