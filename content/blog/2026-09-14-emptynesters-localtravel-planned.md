Both of our sons are off to college now (Texas A&M), so we are officially empty nesters. We have downsized and moved closer to them (Waco). 

It was a busy move time -- 
- August 14th: loaded U-haul
- August 15th: drove to Waco and unloaded U-haul
- August 15th and 16th: stayed at a hotel since they only turn on new water service on Monday, but the weekend U-haul drive through Ft. Worth is much better than during the work week. 
- August 16th: Travis and I drove the U-haul back to the old home for a cheaper rate, finished cleaning and moving the last things from our old house, and drove back. The kids visited the Waco Mammoth National Monument. We took them when they were little, but that was a long time ago.
- August 17th: Went to the new house to wait for the water between 1pm-4pm. They came about 1:20pm, so not bad. Unpack, unpack. The kids were very helpful for the move.
- August 18th: Moved Owen into this dorm at 1pm; Joel helped too.
- August 19th: Visited the Dr. Pepper Museum while Joel was still here. Before, we tried Hawaiian Bros. -- it was really good, especially the pulled pork -- and walked past JoAnna and Chip Gaines's Magnolia. I still need to go back and visit.

We did some Facebook Marketplace shopping to replace some bigger versions that we did not move and versions that would fit and matched better. We got an additional storage cabinet that matched well from Walmart and found a table and four chairs and a sofa on FB. 

Travis has also started preparing our mini-van for roadtripping and camping. We joined the local library. I went to the library craft and joined the Waco Aggies Mom's Club and attended the first meeting. 

We are also working hard to plan some upcoming trips. I sort of found some quick cruises (plural) since we have not cruised yet this year. I found a reasonably priced Royal Caribbean cruise from Galveston that we can drive to without an overnight stay. We have never tried Royal before because they are more expensive; however, I wanted us to try it at some point. It is a 5 night cruise. This is really the first cruise that I only picked for the ship and not the destination. And, hehe, it is not Royal's newest ship by any means -- Mariner of the Seas. No aqua show, but there is an ice skating show :). It is coming up soon. We got a guarantied oceanview and don't have a room assigned yet. It is coming up soon. 

For our Gulf Coast road trip planned, where we planned our first trip to Disney World as empty nesters to be cheaper. Our kids only got a substitute of Legoland CA since they were boys and into Legos. I have to say Disney is CRAZY expensive; it blows my mine. It is our one time trip. Hopefully, it is not crazy busy, and we get a chance to ride the classic rides. For good measure, I included a 4 night cruise before Disney. We are doing the NCL Getaway. We have done 14 NCL cruises but never on one of the newer bigger ships. We have only been on the Jewel class and older. So, we picked a middle aged, bigger ship. It was a very good deal. Hehe -- half of Disney, twice as long, and with the food and hotel included. This is why I'm too addicted to cruising. These are shorter cruises for us, but for trying new line and a side trip, we are going for it. They both start on a Monday too, so should not be booze cruises for a long weekend. 

Lastly, we have made some progress planning our big Asia trip next year.
