Both of our sons are off to college now (Texas A&M), so we are officially empty nesters now. We have downsized and moved closer to them (Waco). 

It was a busy move time -- 
- August 14th: loaded U-haul
- August 15th: drove to Waco and unloaded U-haul
- August 15th and 16th: stayed at a hotel since they only turn on new water service on Monday, but the weekend U-haul drive through Ft. Worth is much better on the weekend. 
- August 16th: Travis and I drove the U-haul back to the old home for a cheaper rate, finished cleaning and moving the last things from our old house, and drove back. The kids visited the Waco Mammoth National Monument. We took them when they were little, but that was a long time ago.
- August 17th: Went to the new house to wait for the water between 1pm-4pm. They came about 1:20pm, so not bad. Unpack, unpack. The kids were very helpful for the move.
- August 18th: Moved Owen into this dorm at 1pm; Joel helped too.
- August 19th: Visited the Dr. Pepper Museum while Joel was still here. Before, we tried Hawaiian Bros. -- it was really good, especially the pulled pork -- and walked past JoAnna and Chip Gaines Magnolia. I still need to go back and visit.

- August 21st: Joel and buddy, each in their own vehicles, planned to leave at 6:30am, but I heard they leaving a little after 6am. They needed to get Corps haircuts and move in. 
- Sept 5th: We went to the pre-game activities -- Joel's Corps BBQ, hangout in Owen's room, hangout with other Corps parents, tailgating, and Corps step-off. We also got to see, for the first time, that the football team arrives in 4-5 big black coach buses with a police escort (I found on their website that there are 99 players and about 80 coach and staff -- crazy!). I wondered where they came from and hoped that they had some cool underground secret entrance. They went around Kyle field, so I could not see where the buses stopped. Joel was also on ABC TV during the game. Thanks to another Corps parent for catching and recording it!
